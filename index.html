The Sikar story

A desert town that became Rajasthan's classroom.

Once known mainly for its coaching institutes, Sikar has quietly grown into one of India's most talked-about education hubs. Families from Jaipur, Delhi and across the Shekhawati region now send their children here for strong board results and early competitive-exam foundations — without the pressure and cost of a metro.

Today the city offers everything from playgroup-to-12 day schools with reliable transport to disciplined residential campuses with hostels, NDA academies and pre-foundation programmes for JEE and NEET.

Our mission

Make school choice in Sikar transparent and free.

Best School Guide Sikar is an independent directory of schools across Sikar city and district. We don't run any school and we don't take money to move a school up the list. Our goal is simple: give parents one trustworthy place to compare their options.

We list schools across every board and stream in Sikar — CBSE, RBSE, ICSE, English medium and pre-foundation — with the information parents actually ask for: real ratings, honest reviews, photos, location and a phone number.

  • 1How we rank. Schools are ordered by the number of Google Maps reviews. More reviews means more families have shared real experiences, which we treat as the strongest signal of a school's footprint in Sikar.
  • 2Where data comes from. Ratings, review counts and comments are drawn from public Google Maps listings. We display them as-is and link out so you can verify.
  • 3Always verify. Fees, timings and affiliation numbers change. Always confirm details directly with the school before admission.
  • 4Get listed or corrected. Run a Sikar school and want to be added or update your details? Reach out and we'll review it.
