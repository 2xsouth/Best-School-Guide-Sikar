The Sikar story

A desert town that became Rajasthan's classroom.

Once known mainly for its coaching institutes, Sikar has quietly grown into one of India's most talked-about education hubs. Families from Jaipur, Delhi and across the Shekhawati region now send their children here for strong board results and early competitive-exam foundations.

Today the city offers everything from playgroup-to-12 day schools with reliable transport to disciplined residential campuses with hostels, NDA academies and pre-foundation programmes for JEE and NEET.
