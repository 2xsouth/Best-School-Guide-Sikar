About Best School Guide Sikar

Why this guide exists, how it works, and how we rank schools.

Our mission

Make school choice in Sikar transparent and free.

Best School Guide Sikar is an independent directory of schools across Sikar city and district. We list schools across every board and stream — CBSE, RBSE, ICSE, English medium and pre-foundation — with the information parents actually ask for: ratings, reviews, photos, location and a phone number.

  • 1How we rank. Schools are ordered by the number of Google Maps reviews — more reviews means more families have shared real experiences.
  • 2Where data comes from. Ratings, review counts and comments are drawn from public Google Maps listings. Always verify with the school.
  • 3Featured school. Euro International School is our featured campus.
  • 4Get listed. Run a Sikar school and want to be added or corrected? Reach out and we'll review it.
